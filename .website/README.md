## 🌈 One verb, four uses

Hold **`Shift`** and your horn hoses out a *solid* rainbow. The head of the stream flies forward, falls under its own gravity, and lays a rainbow behind it until it splashes against the scenery.

**Where you fire from is what it becomes:**

| | Fired from | What you get |
| :-: | --- | --- |
| 💥 | Flat ground | a sweep that purifies the Gloom in front of you |
| 🌉 | The edge of a drop | an arc across the gap — **a bridge you can stand on** |
| 📈 | A rising jump | a climbing ramp |
| 🎈 | Mid-air | lift, because the stream pushes back |

That's the whole game. Thirteen courses, and none of them give you a second verb — they just keep finding new questions for the one you have.

---

## ⏱️ A speedrun you can't lose

One clock, and **it never stops**. Falling in the lava costs you *time*, not lives — the level snaps back instantly and the run keeps going. There's no game over, only a worse time.

Your rainbows **fade**, so keep moving. Wisps drink any rainbow they can reach. One hit is fatal.
